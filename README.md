# Cue And Cadence Coach

We are making an application: everything has to work in and audio out. no screens, no tapping, no reading. The functions are: Similar to a personal trainer- remianding us to breathe in and out, details we tend to forget and where we should feel the stress/burn, tighten your core, correct posture, talk to the app tell what workout we are doing/focusing on and the app starts and gives us the feedback/intrsuctions. The AI is telling us where we should feel it and responds to our questions.
